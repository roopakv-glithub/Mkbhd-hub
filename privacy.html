Privacy & data

This fan-made site has no analytics or advertising scripts. Here is what the forms and interactive features save, and how you can clear it.

What this site saves

  • Form entries: With server storage configured, the newsletter form sends your name, email, topic, and consent; the fan form sends your name, email, submission type, message, and consent to a private Supabase database. They are not emailed or publicly displayed. A local recovery copy is also kept in this browser.
  • Shared poll: Vote totals are stored on the server and shared across visitors. A random, HTTP-only browser cookie lets you change or remove your vote. The database stores a hash of that cookie, not its value.
  • Fan wall: The wall still displays only entries saved in this browser; submissions aren't automatically published to other visitors.
  • Clear data: The button below removes this browser's locally saved entries and, while its browser cookie is available, its server submissions and poll vote. Clearing cookies first prevents the site from identifying which server entries belong to this browser.
  • External services: The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, images from Unsplash, and an embedded video from YouTube. Those services may receive your browser's request data when their content loads. Vercel hosts the site/API and Supabase stores server entries; each may process service request data.

Newsletter emails and submission updates are not sent because no email delivery service is configured. Newsletter signups and fan submissions can only be stored when the server database has been configured.
