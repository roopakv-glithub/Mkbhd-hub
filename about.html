About · The Story

From a bedroom in Jersey to the biggest tech stage on Earth.

Marques Keith Brownlee started uploading in 2008 — screencasts, golf videos, then one phone review that changed everything. This is the timeline fans love to retell.

The ethos

Calm. Crisp. Honest.

While tech YouTube got louder, Marques went quieter — slower delivery, harder testing, cleaner shots. That contrast became the brand: the guy every company fears and every viewer trusts.

  • Ultimate frisbee pro — played for the Philadelphia Spinners while building the channel.
  • Stevens Institute grad — studied business & IT, edited between classes.
  • Interviewer of legends — Gates, Musk, Obama, Kobe, Gates… twice.
2008

The first upload

A teenager posts freeware screencasts from Maplewood, New Jersey. 100 videos in before anyone notices.

2013

1 million subscribers

The Moto X review and LG G Flex go mega-viral. Google's Vic Gundotra calls him "the best technology reviewer on the planet."

2014

Smartphone Awards begin

The annual awards show launches — now the most anticipated phone video of every year.

2018

10M subs + Kobe interview

Passes 10 million subscribers and interviews Elon Musk, Will Smith, and Kobe Bryant in the same era.

2021

Waveform & Studio expand

The Waveform podcast and Studio channel turn MKBHD from a channel into a media team.

2026

19M+ and still matte black

Pannels, Panels app, dbrand collabs, Atoms shoes — and still the calmest verdict on the internet.
